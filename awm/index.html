Directions

  • Place the band: drag players from the wings onto the stage. Their instrument appears once they're on stage, drawn at its real played size.
  • Face a player: select them and press R / Shift+R (or ⟲ ⟳). The arrow on their ring shows which way they face; the instrument swings with them.
  • Swap instruments: select a player and click what they're holding, or press I. Tick “Plays” to change what they play; “Left-handed” mirrors the instrument.
  • Worst case for swappers: turn on “Show swappers' other instrument” to see and check both at once.
  • Add gear: click a button on the right; it lands in the New gear row. Drag it on stage, rotate with R.
  • Rename: double-click any icon.
  • Delete gear: select it, then click the red ✕ or press Delete.
  • Nudge: arrow keys move the selection 3″.

Warnings

  • Red ring — overlapping something. Necks, bodies and the fiddle's bow sweep count.
  • Orange ring — hanging off the edge of the stage.
  • Tripod stands only count at the center pole, since players stand among the legs.

Saving

  • Your layout auto-saves in this browser.
  • Save downloads the layout as a file you can keep or share; Load opens one. For a picture, take a screenshot.
