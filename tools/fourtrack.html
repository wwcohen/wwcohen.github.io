  • Use headphones while overdubbing, or the mic will pick up the other tracks.
  • Arm a track (R button), then press ● to record. Recording drops in at the playhead and keeps whatever was after the point where you stop, like tape.
  • Latency moves new takes earlier so they line up with what you heard. To measure it, unplug your headphones, turn the speakers up, keep the room quiet and press Calibrate. If takes still sound late, increase the value; if they sound early, decrease it.
  • Click a waveform to move the playhead. Tracks are saved in this browser and are still there after a reload.
  • Keys: Space play/stop · R record · Home return to start.
